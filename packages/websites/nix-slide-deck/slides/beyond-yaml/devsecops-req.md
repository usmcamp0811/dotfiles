# What DevSecOps Actually Requires

:: content ::

- **Enforce security and compliance policies during the build** — not after deployment.  
  *Aligned with NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: CM-3 (Configuration Change Control), CM-6 (Configuration Settings), and SA-8 (Security and Privacy Engineering Principles).*

- **Prove exactly what source, dependencies, and tools produced every artifact.**  
  *Aligned with NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: SA-8(22) (Accountability and Traceability).*

- **Guarantee identical builds from identical inputs — on any machine, at any time.**  
  *Aligned with NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: CM-6 (Configuration Settings) and SI-12 (Information Handling and Retention).*

- **Automatically generate SBOMs and logs — every build is audit-ready by default.**  
  *Aligned with NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5: AU-2 (Audit Events), AU-6 (Audit Review, Analysis, and Reporting), and AU-11 (Audit Record Retention).*

YAML alone can’t deliver this
